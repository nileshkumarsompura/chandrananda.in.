ShilpShastra Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ShilpShastra handles information when you use the Android application. By using the app, you acknowledge the practices described below.

Information we process

  • Account information: depending on your sign-in method, Firebase Authentication may process your name, email address, phone number, profile photo, account identifier and authentication provider.
  • App data: settings, selected language, reminders, notification read status, saved preferences and feature usage.
  • Location: approximate or precise location is used only when required for location-based Panchang, sunrise/sunset or compass features and when you grant permission.
  • Calculator and membership data: calculator requests, results, daily usage counters, membership status, order identifiers and payment confirmation references may be stored securely using Firebase.
  • Diagnostics: device and app diagnostic information may be processed to protect the service, investigate errors and prevent abuse.

How we use information

  • Authenticate users and maintain their accounts.
  • Provide Panchang, calculator, reminder and membership features.
  • Show relevant in-app and system notifications.
  • Process and verify payments through Razorpay.
  • Protect the app, prevent fraud and improve reliability.
  • Respond to support, privacy and account requests.

Service providers

ShilpShastra uses service providers such as Google Firebase for authentication, database, app integrity and backend processing, and Razorpay for payment processing. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Payment card, bank and UPI credentials are entered in Razorpay's checkout and are not stored by ShilpShastra.

Storage and security

We use reasonable technical safeguards, including authenticated Firebase access, backend authorization, App Check and encrypted Android storage where applicable. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Information is retained only as long as reasonably required to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and prevent abuse.

Your choices

  • You may deny or revoke location and notification permissions.
  • You may change the app language and reminder preferences in Settings.
  • You may sign out at any time.
  • You may request access, correction or deletion of account-related data by contacting the support address below. Some records may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and accounting needs.

Children's privacy

ShilpShastra is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child provided personal information without appropriate consent, contact us so we can review and remove it where required.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or legal requirements change. The current effective date will be shown at the top of this page.
